Isolated workspaces

Every read is scoped to your firm’s workspace on the server. Another firm’s data is never one bad link away.

Invitation-only

No one joins a workspace by signing up with the right email domain. Every member arrives by invitation.

Read-only client seats

Clients see only the mandates they’re attached to. Access is checked on every request, not remembered.

Spreadsheets stay out of the model

When AI helps map a spreadsheet it sees column headers and value shapes — never the names, salaries or notes in the cells.
