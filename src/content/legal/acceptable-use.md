This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/terms). It applies to everyone using Uncava, including the browser extension and connected mailboxes.

## Use Uncava for executive search

Uncava is for researching markets, finding and assessing executives, and approaching them about genuine roles. Do not use it for anything else, such as marketing, sales prospecting, debt collection, or building a contact list to resell.

## Contact people responsibly

- Contact executives only about real opportunities and only where you have a lawful basis to do so.
- Make every outreach email identify you and your firm truthfully, and honour every reply asking not to be contacted. Uncava's "do not contact" flag must be set when someone asks.
- Do not send bulk or unsolicited commercial email, use purchased lists, or disguise who is sending.
- Keep within the sending limits Uncava and your email provider set. Do not try to work around them.

## Respect the data

- Use data supplied by Uncava only inside your own search work. Do not export it in bulk, publish it, resell it or use it to train models.
- Do not use Uncava to discriminate against anyone on grounds such as gender, nationality, religion, age or disability. AI-derived attributes are for diversity reporting and research, not for excluding people.
- Do not upload information you have no right to hold, or special categories of data (such as health or religious belief) that the search does not need.
- Respect off-limits lists agreed with your clients.

## Don't misuse the service

- Do not access another workspace's data, probe or test the service's security without our written permission, or bypass access controls, rate limits or AI budgets.
- Do not use the Capture extension or the service to scrape websites in breach of their terms, or automate the service except through features we provide.
- Do not attempt to manipulate the AI features into ignoring their safeguards (prompt injection).
- Do not upload malware or anything unlawful, defamatory or infringing.
- Do not share your account or let anyone outside your organisation use it.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report misuse or a security issue to [Security email address]. We may investigate suspected breaches of this policy and suspend or remove access, content or connected accounts where necessary, telling you why unless the law prevents it.
