## Why you are hearing about Uncava

Uncava is software that executive search firms and in-house hiring teams use to research the market for senior
roles. To do that, it obtains professional profiles from licensed business data providers. For the profiles it
obtains and keeps itself, Uncava decides how they are used, so it is responsible for them and owes you this notice.
A firm that saves you to one of its own records is responsible for that record, and tells you about it itself.

## What we hold, and where it comes from

Your name, current and past roles and employers, location, education and your public professional profile, from
licensed business data providers (Bright Data, ContactOut and HarvestAPI) and your public LinkedIn profile. Where a
firm asks, a business email address and phone number. Never sensitive information about you.

## Why we hold it

So that firms can find executives who fit a role, which is in their interest and, we believe, in yours: it is how
senior roles reach the people who could fill them. [Lawful basis to be confirmed by counsel for each jurisdiction.]

## How long we keep it

- Profiles Uncava fetches from a provider are kept for [30] days, so the same profile is not fetched twice.
- A firm that holds you without your consent and has never contacted you deletes your record after 6 months.
- Once a firm has contacted you, or you have agreed to stay on its file, it keeps your record for the periods it
  tells you.

## Your choices

You can ask to see what is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to it being used. Every email
sent to you through Uncava carries a link to stop all contact, and that request always wins. To ask Uncava about the
profiles it keeps itself, write to [Privacy email address]. For a firm's record about you, write to the firm: its
details are in every email it sends you through Uncava.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. [Authorities to be named by counsel.]
