This addendum forms part of the agreement between [Company legal name] ("Uncava") and the customer that uses Uncava ("you"). It applies when Uncava processes personal data on your behalf. A countersigned copy is available on request from [Legal email address].

## Roles

You are the controller of the personal data your workspace puts into Uncava ("customer personal data"). Uncava is your processor. Uncava is a separate controller for its own account, billing and security data, and for the company and executive data it sources itself, as described in the [Privacy policy](/privacy).

## Scope of processing

- **Subject matter:** providing the Uncava executive search service.
- **Duration:** the term of the agreement, plus the deletion period below.
- **Data subjects:** executives and candidates, client contacts, and your users.
- **Data:** names, contact details, professional history, compensation and notice period, documents, notes, AI assessments and derived attributes, outreach emails and meeting details.
- **Nature:** storage, search, analysis (including AI), transmission of email and calendar invitations you initiate, and reporting.

## Instructions

Uncava processes customer personal data only on your documented instructions — the agreement, this addendum and your use of the service — unless the law requires otherwise, in which case Uncava tells you first unless the law forbids it.

## Confidentiality and security

Everyone at Uncava with access to customer personal data is bound by confidentiality. Uncava keeps the technical and organisational measures described on the [Security](/security) page, including encryption in transit, encryption of stored credentials, workspace isolation, role-based access and audit logging.

## Sub-processors

You authorise the sub-processors listed on the [Sub-processors](/subprocessors) page. Uncava binds each to data protection terms no less protective than these and remains responsible for them. Uncava gives notice before adding or replacing one; you may object on reasonable data protection grounds within [30] days, and if we cannot resolve it you may end the affected service.

## International transfers

Customer personal data is hosted in the United States. Uncava transfers it only under a mechanism the applicable law allows, including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and the GDPR [mechanism to confirm].

## Assistance

Uncava helps you, taking into account the nature of the processing, to respond to data subject requests, carry out data protection impact assessments and meet your security obligations. Uncava passes on any request it receives directly from a data subject about your data.

## Personal data breaches

Uncava notifies you without undue delay, and within [72] hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting customer personal data, with the information you need to meet your own obligations.

## Deletion and return

You can export your data at any time. At the end of the agreement Uncava deletes customer personal data within [90] days, unless the law requires it to be kept.

Uncava makes available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this addendum and answers your reasonable security questionnaires. [On-site audit terms to confirm.]
