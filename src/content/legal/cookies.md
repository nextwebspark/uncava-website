Uncava uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, on the website or in the application. Because everything below is strictly necessary for the service to work, there is nothing to opt into and no cookie banner.

## On uncava.com

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Fonts are served from uncava.com itself.

The editor at uncava.com/admin, used only by our own staff, keeps its sign-in in browser storage.

## In the Uncava application

| Name                 | Type   | Purpose                                                                        | Lasts      |
| -------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| `lm_refresh`         | Cookie | Keeps you signed in. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict; replaced on every use. | 30 days    |
| `lm_oauth_request`   | Cookie | Protects a Google or LinkedIn sign-in while it completes.                      | 10 minutes |
| `lm_mailbox_connect` | Cookie | Protects connecting a Gmail or Microsoft mailbox while it completes.           | 10 minutes |
| `lm_zoom_connect`    | Cookie | Protects connecting a Zoom account while it completes.                         | 10 minutes |

The application also uses your browser's local storage for display preferences — theme, panel and section layout, talent map and list views — and session storage for where to return after signing in. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

The application loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser's IP address reaches Google when a page loads; Google does not set cookies for this.

## In the Capture extension

The UNCAVA Capture extension keeps your sign-in token in the browser's extension storage for up to 14 days. It sets no cookies.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cookies above, you will not be able to sign in to the application.
