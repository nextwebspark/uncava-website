Uncava Capture is a Chrome extension. It files a company or an executive from the page you are reading straight into one of your mandates, without copying and pasting between tabs.

:::note[Before you start]
You need a Lead or Researcher seat on the mandate you want to capture into, and Google Chrome [supported Chrome versions].
:::

## Install

1. **Open the Chrome Web Store listing**
   Go to [Chrome Web Store link] and select **Add to Chrome**.
2. **Pin the extension**
   Open Chrome’s extensions menu and pin **Uncava Capture** so it stays in your toolbar.
3. **Connect your account**
   Open the extension and connect it to your Uncava account. [Describe the connection screen and what the user approves.]

## Capture a company or an executive

1. **Open the page you are researching**
   [List the kinds of pages the extension reads.]
2. **Open Uncava Capture**
   The extension shows what it found on the page — a company, or an executive and their company.
3. **Choose the mandate**
   Pick the mandate to capture into.
4. **Add to mandate**
   The company or executive appears in the mandate’s **Companies** pages, marked as captured.

## Troubleshooting

[Common problems and their fixes, once known.]

Captured something by mistake? Remove it from the mandate’s **Companies** page like any other row.
