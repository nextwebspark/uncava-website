This guide walks through one mandate from start to finish. It assumes you are a member of a workspace and can create mandates in it.

:::note[Before you start]
Have the client’s position description to hand if there is one. Uncava can read it and propose fields for the brief.
:::

## Set up the mandate

1. **Create the mandate**
   Create a project for the client company and give it the role title — for example “Group CFO – Energy Division”. The title does not need to match a standard job title.
2. **Add your team**
   On the mandate’s **Team & access** tab, give each colleague a seat: **Lead** for the person who owns the mandate, **Researcher** for everyone working on it.

## Write the brief

The **Position** screen is the mandate’s brief. It opens already drafted from the role template that best matches your role title, and every step saves as you edit it.

1. **Check the role title**
   The role title field suggests the titles in the template library as you type. Picking one takes that title and redrafts the brief from its template.
2. **Read from the position description**
   Attach the client’s document in step one and choose **Read from document**. Uncava proposes values for the step’s fields, each with a confidence and the passage it came from. Accept or dismiss each one; nothing is written until you accept it.
3. **Work through the steps**
   Details, mandate context, reporting, compensation, assessment and review. In reporting, edit the org chart around the role’s own seat: add, rename and re-parent the seats around it.
4. **Publish the brief**
   Publishing records who called the brief ready. It does not lock it — you can keep editing afterwards.

## Build the market

1. **Filter the company universe**
   In **Strategy**, filter more than 100,000 companies by industry, size and geography. Save the searches you want to come back to.
2. **File companies**
   Add a company to the mandate from its row, or tick several and use the bar that appears to file them all at one stage — **In universe**, **Shortlisted** or **Declined** — in one step.

## Decide and map

1. **Triage in Companies**
   The **Companies** pages hold the three stages. Move each company to the stage that reflects your decision. Companies you find elsewhere can be added by hand, captured with the [browser extension](/docs/capture/install-the-extension), or [imported from a spreadsheet](/docs/companies/import-a-spreadsheet).
2. **Add executives**
   Each row is a person at a company, so a company with three executives shows three lines. Use a company’s **Add executive** slot to map someone there.
3. **Look at the map**
   On the **In universe** page, switch from **Table** to **Map** to see a panel of countries, companies and executives beside a globe, with a pin for each company and executive.

## Bring the client in

1. **Invite a client representative**
   From the mandate’s **Team & access** tab, attach a representative of the hiring company. They get a read-only **Client** seat on the mandates they are attached to and cannot see any other search.

:::caution[Client seats are read-only]
A client representative can follow the mandate’s brief, companies and executives, but cannot change anything. Invite them only once the content is ready to be seen.
:::
