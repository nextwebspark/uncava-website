Uncava is a workspace for executive search and talent mapping. A search firm runs each mandate in it: the brief for the role, the market of companies the search covers, the decisions made about each company, and the executives mapped at them.

## The words Uncava uses

| Term      | What it means                                                                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Workspace | Your firm. Everything in Uncava belongs to exactly one workspace, and no workspace can see another’s data.               |
| Member    | A person in your workspace. Every member arrives by invitation; nobody joins by signing up with a matching email domain. |
| Mandate   | A search for a client company. Uncava also calls it a project.                                                           |
| Seat      | A person’s place on a mandate: **Lead** owns the mandate, **Researcher** works on it, **Client** can only read it.       |
| Position  | The mandate’s brief — what the role is, why it exists, what it pays and how candidates are assessed.                     |
| Strategy  | The search over a universe of more than 100,000 companies, filtered by industry, size and geography.                     |
| Companies | The companies a mandate has taken from the market, each filed at one of three stages.                                    |
| Executive | A person mapped on a mandate, optionally against one of its companies.                                                   |

## Roles in a workspace

A member of a workspace holds one or more of three roles:

- **Admin** manages the workspace and its members.
- **Member** is a consultant or researcher at your firm.
- **Client** is a representative of a hiring company. A client representative has read-only access to the mandates they are attached to, and nothing else.

What a person can do on a particular mandate comes from their seat on it, not from their workspace role alone.

## How a search runs

Uncava follows the order a consultant works a mandate:

1. **Write the brief**
   The Position screen is a six-step wizard — details, mandate context, reporting, compensation, assessment and review — that saves as you go. It opens drafted from a library of role templates, and it can read the client’s position description and propose fields for you to accept.
2. **Search the market**
   Strategy filters the company universe and saves the searches the mandate runs on. Tick companies to file them all at one stage in a single step.
3. **Decide on every company**
   Companies holds the three stages: **In universe**, **Shortlisted** and **Declined**. A company can also arrive by hand, from the Uncava Capture browser extension, or from a spreadsheet import.
4. **Map the executives**
   Each row in Companies is a person at a company. A company with no executives yet keeps an **Add executive** slot. Switch from **Table** to **Map** to see companies and executives by country and city.
