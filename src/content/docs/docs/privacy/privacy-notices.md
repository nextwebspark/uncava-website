Most people in your candidate pool were sourced: nobody asked them first. Data protection law expects your firm to tell them within a month, or at first contact, that you hold their details, and to know the lawful basis you hold them on. Uncava keeps both records on each candidate and sends the notice for you.

:::note[Before you start]
An admin has to [set up candidate privacy](/docs/privacy/set-up-candidate-privacy) first. Any **Admin** or **Member** can send notices. Client representatives never see any of this.
:::

## A candidate’s privacy record

Open a candidate under **Candidates**. The **Privacy** card on their **Profile** tab shows:

- **Lawful basis**: the one a colleague recorded, or your **Workspace default**. Change it here, and the candidate’s activity records who changed it and when.
- **Notified** or **Not notified**: whether the person has been told, when, and how. They might have been told by a privacy notice, by their first outreach email, or another way that a colleague recorded.

## Send a privacy notice

1. **To one person**
   In the drawer’s **⋯** menu, or on the **Privacy** card, choose **Send privacy notice**.
2. **To many**
   Select people in **Candidates** and choose **Send privacy notice** from the bar that appears. One send can cover up to 500 people.

The email arrives as “_your firm_ via Uncava”, and replies go to your privacy contact. It names your firm as the controller and says where the person’s details came from and the lawful basis you hold them on. It links to your privacy notice, and has a button that opens the person’s own privacy page.

Uncava skips a person, and tells you who it skipped and why, if they:

- have no email address on file,
- are marked do not contact,
- have processing restricted,
- are on your suppression list,
- were sent a notice in the last 14 days, or
- would go over your workspace’s daily sending limit.

If the email is refused on its way out, the person goes back to **Not notified** and you can send it again straight away.

## Told another way

If someone was told in person, on a call or in a letter, choose **Mark as notified** and enter the day. Uncava keeps only the day, not what was said.

## Who has never been told

The **Not notified** quick view in **Candidates** lists everyone you have held for more than 30 days who has never been told. Nothing sends a notice on its own: the list shows who you still need to act on.

## What the person sees

The notice links to a private page, valid for 30 days. It does not need a sign-in. Under **What we hold about you, and your choices**, it shows:

- **Why we may hold it**: your firm and the lawful basis.
- **Where it came from**: how the person came to be in your workspace.
- **What we hold**: the _kinds_ of data held, such as name and role, career history, work email and notes. It never shows the values, or even the person’s name, because a link can be forwarded.

Under **Your choices**, the person can pick one of three, and each asks once more before it goes through:

| Choice                        | What happens                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Don’t contact me again**    | The person is marked do not contact and put on your suppression list straight away. Every admin gets a notification and an email. |
| **Send me a copy of my data** | Every admin gets a notification and an email. An admin answers with the subject access export.                                    |
| **Delete my data**            | Every admin gets a notification and an email. An admin answers by erasing the person.                                             |

Uncava never exports or deletes anything from this page by itself. A request has a one-month deadline, so its notification is emailed at once rather than saved for the digest. An expired link still names your firm and its privacy contact, so the person can write to you.
