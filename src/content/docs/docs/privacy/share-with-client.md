When your firm offers **Share my profile with a client for a specific role** as a consent purpose, you can ask a candidate whether their profile may be shown to a position’s client, one position at a time. Depending on how your workspace is set up, it may call the client a business unit.

:::note[Before you start]
An admin has to turn on the share purpose in [candidate privacy](/docs/privacy/set-up-candidate-privacy). You need a **Lead** or **Researcher** seat on the position.
:::

## Ask to share

You can ask from any of three places:

- **The Pipeline board**: on a candidate’s card, open **⋯** and choose **Ask to share with client…**.
- **The candidate drawer**: in **Ask for consent**, tick the share purpose and pick the position. The drawer lists only the positions you work on.
- **The visibility prompt**: choose **Ask first** (see below).

A share request names one position, so it is never sent to a whole selection at once.

## Naming the client

By default, the email and the candidate’s page say “a confidential client”. Tick **Name the client** to name it. If the position’s brief is marked confidential, Uncava never lets you name the client.

## The Pipeline board

Each card on the position’s **Pipeline** board shows where its share consent stands: **No consent to share**, **Consent asked** or **Consent given**. Candidates are visible to the client by default, so in a workspace that asks, most cards start with **No consent to share** until you ask.

## Turning visibility on without consent

Turning a candidate visible to the client, on their Pipeline card or with **Client visible** in Outreach, opens a prompt when they have not given share consent: **No consent to share with _client_ yet**.

- **Ask first** sends the request. From Outreach, it always uses the confidential wording. To name the client, ask from the Pipeline card.
- **Show anyway** makes the candidate visible. The candidate’s activity and the audit trail record that they were shown without consent.
- **Cancel** leaves the candidate as they were.

Uncava never blocks you. Hiding a candidate never asks.

:::caution[A declined or withdrawn share does not hide anyone]
If a candidate declines or withdraws consent to share, they stay visible to the client until someone hides them. The badge changes and the person who asked gets a notification. Hide the candidate yourself.
:::

## The position’s consent register

On the mandate’s **Companies** page, choose **Consent register** under **Data** in the toolbar menu. It downloads every share request and answer for that position as a CSV, and the download is recorded in the audit log. The workspace-wide register, under **Settings → Data & retention**, includes these shares too.
