Every outreach email sent from Uncava ends with a short privacy footer, after your signature. That covers the first email of a sequence, every follow-up, and an email a consultant writes themselves. Nobody can edit the footer or remove it.

## What it says

> _Your firm_ holds your professional details, which came from _where they came from_. Our privacy notice · Stop emails from us

Until an admin [sets up candidate privacy](/docs/privacy/set-up-candidate-privacy), the footer says “We hold …” in place of your firm’s name.

- **Our privacy notice** opens your firm’s privacy notice, or the person’s own privacy page if no notice link is set.
- **Stop emails from us** opens a page that asks once, “Stop emails from _your firm_?”. It asks first because some mail scanners open every link in an email, and a scanner must never opt anyone out.

You see the footer, read-only, under the email in the sequence preview, in **Add to sequence → Review**, and in the composer. It is added as the email leaves, so the conversation in Uncava shows your message without it.

## One-click unsubscribe

Emails sent from a Gmail mailbox, and through most connected providers, also carry the standard one-click unsubscribe header. Gmail and other mail apps then show their own **Unsubscribe** button. A Microsoft 365 or Outlook mailbox cannot add that header, so its emails rely on the footer’s link alone.

## When someone opts out

Opting out, either from the footer or from their privacy page, takes effect at once:

- The person is marked do not contact, with the reason “Opted out by email”, and put on your suppression list.
- Every running sequence on them stops, shown as **Stopped · Opted out**, and any email still waiting to go is cancelled.
- The person’s owner, or whoever sent the email, gets a notification.

## The first email counts as a notice

Once your firm’s name, privacy contact and notice link are saved, the footer carries everything a privacy notice must say. So when someone who was never told before receives their first outreach email, Uncava records them as notified by that email. If they were told earlier, that earlier record stands.
