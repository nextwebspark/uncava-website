This page is about your own Uncava account and your firm’s agreement with Uncava, not about the candidates you hold.

## Accepting the Terms

Every way into a new account asks you to tick **I agree to the Terms and the Privacy Policy**. The box is never ticked for you, and no account is created until you tick it.

- **Signing up with an email and password**: the box sits on the signup form.
- **Accepting an invitation**: the box sits on the form where you set your password.
- **A first sign-in with Google or LinkedIn**: Uncava asks on a step of its own, before the account is created. If you leave the step for more than ten minutes, sign in with the provider again.

Signing in to an account you already have asks nothing.

## When the Terms change

When the Terms or the Privacy Policy change, a sheet covers Uncava the next time you sign in, until you accept the new version or sign out. **What changed** opens the updated terms. The version you accepted and the time you accepted it are recorded on your account.

## The data processing agreement

Uncava processes candidate data on your firm’s behalf, under a data processing agreement (DPA). Once a DPA version is published, whoever founds a workspace accepts it for the firm. That applies both at signup and under **Settings → Workspaces → New workspace**. Uncava records the time, the version and who accepted, and keeps that record even if the workspace is later deleted.
