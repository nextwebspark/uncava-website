Your firm is the controller of the data it holds about the people it sources, so the people it holds have to be told about it. Uncava sends those notices and consent requests **in your firm’s name**. Before it can, an admin saves your firm’s details once, in **Settings → Data & retention → Candidate privacy**.

:::note[Before you start]
You need the **Admin** role to change these settings. Any **Admin** or **Member** can read them. Have your firm’s legal name, the mailbox that answers privacy questions, and the address of your own privacy notice ready.
:::

## Save your firm’s details

1. **Open Candidate privacy**
   Go to **Settings → Data & retention** and find the **Candidate privacy** card.
2. **Firm’s legal name**
   This name appears as the controller in every privacy notice and outreach email.
3. **Privacy contact email**
   Replies to a privacy notice or a consent request go to this address.
4. **Privacy notice link**
   The `https://` address of your firm’s own privacy notice. Every notice and outreach email links to it.
5. **Default lawful basis**
   **Legitimate interest**, **Consent** or **Contract**. Every candidate gets this basis unless a colleague records a different one on them.
6. **Save**

Uncava sends no privacy notice or consent request until the name, the contact and the link are all saved. Saving changes nothing anyone can see until your team starts sending.

## Choose a mode

After the details are saved you can pick a **Mode**:

| Mode                             | What it does                                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notices**                      | Candidates are told that you hold their details. Nobody is asked for consent.      |
| **Notices and consent requests** | Candidates are told, and you can also ask them for consent to one purpose or more. |

With **Notices and consent requests**, you choose the purposes your firm asks about:

| Purpose                                                | What it covers                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Keep my details on file for future roles**           | You may keep someone’s details for roles beyond the current one. **Valid for** sets how long consent lasts: 12, 24 or 36 months. While it is in force, the candidate is kept beyond your retention period. |
| **Share my profile with a client for a specific role** | You may share someone’s profile with a client for one position. See [Share a profile with a client](/docs/privacy/share-with-client).                                                                      |
| **Career and market updates**                          | Uncava only records the answer. It sends no marketing email.                                                                                                                                               |

**Days before consent ends** sets how many days before a keep-on-file consent runs out Uncava asks the person once more, anywhere from 0 to 90. Enter 0 and Uncava never asks again.

## Download the consent register

In consent mode, the card offers **Download consent register**. It is a CSV with every request and answer in the workspace. Each line has the person’s name, the purpose, the position for a share, its status, when it was asked and who asked, when and how it was answered, how long it lasts, and the exact wording the person saw. It never contains an email address or a phone number.

:::note[Don’t see Candidate privacy?]
If **Data & retention** shows only **AI on people**, data protection controls have not been turned on for your workspace yet. Ask [support contact] to turn them on.
:::
